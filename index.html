Kerala has traded pepper and spice for over two thousand years. We still do it the honest way.

No anonymous middlemen, no blended lots of unknown origin — just the same hill-to-port relationship that's defined Malabar's spice trade for generations, run to a modern standard.

Our Origin

Born in the Western Ghats, Traded with Honesty

Wayanad sits high in Kerala's Western Ghats — cool, misty, and rich with the biodiversity that gives its produce an intensity you won't find grown elsewhere. D A TRADERS works directly with the farmers of Nadavayal and the surrounding hills to bring that character to buyers, unaltered.

No blending. No shortcuts. Every batch is graded and packed close to where it's grown — so what reaches you is exactly what left the hill.

White Pepper — Production & Grades

Produced by retting fully ripe red pepper berries in water to loosen and remove the outer skin (pericarp), leaving the clean white inner seed — a slower, more selective process than black pepper production.

GradeDensityNotes

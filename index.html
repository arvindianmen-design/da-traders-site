The Wayanad Vista

Cool mist over the Western Ghats, every morning of the year

Wayanad sits over 700 metres up, where the fog settles into the valleys until mid-morning and the air stays cool and humid year-round — the exact conditions that give this land its character, and its produce theirs.

Our Origin

Born in the Western Ghats, Traded with Honesty

Wayanad sits high in Kerala's Western Ghats — cool, misty, and rich with the biodiversity that gives its produce an intensity you won't find grown elsewhere. D A TRADERS works directly with the farmers of Nadavayal and the surrounding hills to bring that character to buyers, unaltered.

No blending. No shortcuts. Every batch is graded and packed close to where it's grown — so what reaches you is exactly what left the hill.

Technical Specifications

Roast Profiles & Export Moisture Standard

Every batch roasted and graded against a documented profile — not eyeballed.

Light Roast

180°C – 205°C

Light brown. High acidity, floral notes, light body — origin character most visible.

Medium Roast

210°C – 220°C

Medium brown. Balanced sweetness, medium body, optimal aroma — the standard filter roast.

Dark Roast

225°C – 235°C

Dark brown, shiny oil. Bold, low acidity, smoky notes, rich crema — built for espresso.

Our Origin & Farm Supply Network

Direct Sourcing from Wayanad's High-Altitude Belts

D A TRADERS operates directly at origin in Nadavayal, Wayanad (Western Ghats, altitude 700m–1100m MSL). We source raw agricultural produce straight from our network of local smallholders and estate farmers, eliminating intermediary traders to guarantee single-origin authenticity.

Sourcing runs across the Nadavayal, Panamaram, Kalpetta and Sultan Bathery agricultural belts, with lot-level tracking from estate harvest through to final packing. We follow ISO 22000-aligned hygiene practices and farm-level pesticide compliance on every lot we handle.

White Pepper — Production & Grades

Produced by double-retting fully ripe red pepper berries in water to loosen and remove the outer skin (pericarp), leaving the clean white inner seed — a slower, more selective process than black pepper production.
